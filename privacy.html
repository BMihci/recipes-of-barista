  • Google AdMob and Google User Messaging Platform – rewarded ads and ad consent. How Google uses data

These providers may store data outside Turkey and the EEA (e.g. in the USA). Transfers rely on the providers' Standard Contractual Clauses and equivalent safeguards. We do not sell your data.

4. Ad consent

In the EU, EEA, UK and Switzerland, Google's consent form is shown on first launch. If you decline personalised ads, only non-personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time under Settings → Legal & Account → Ad privacy options, and reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings. A banner is shown at the bottom of the game screen and an interstitial ad after about 2 minutes of uninterrupted play; watching rewarded videos is optional. Owners of Ad-Free Game or the Supporter Pack see no ads at all.

5. Retention

On-device data stays until you delete it. Account, cloud save, leaderboard and invite records are kept until you delete your account and are erased within 30 days of a deletion request. Purchase records are retained by Google Play as required by law.

6. Your rights

You have the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict, object to processing, data portability and to withdraw consent at any time. Send requests to omermihci94@gmail.com; we answer within 30 days. You may also lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority (in Turkey: the Personal Data Protection Authority, KVKK).

7. Deleting your data

  • In the game: Profile → Delete my account (account, cloud save, leaderboard entry, invite code).
  • On-device data: Settings → Delete all data, or uninstall the app.
  • Without the app: account deletion page.

8. Children

Because the game contains slot machine mechanics (simulated gambling), it is not directed at anyone under 18 and asks for age confirmation on first launch. If we learn that we hold data of a person under 18, we delete it.

9. Security

Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). Cloud data can only be accessed with your own account (Firestore security rules).

10. Changes

We may update this policy. Significant changes are announced in the game; the current version is always on this page.
